# Candidate office jokes — The Funniest Office Joke (u-kevin, 2026-11-25)

All original, written for this competition. Judge brief: one line, setup + turn,
built on something anyone who has worked in an office recognises, punchline last,
surprising, fresh. Gate: 1–200 characters.

## Candidates

1. "My boss says we're a family here, so I asked for my inheritance — he gave me his old laptop and put me on a performance improvement plan."
2. "Our manager said the door is always open, so I walked out through it at five — apparently that is not what he meant."
3. "The office printer only jams when the deadline is close, which is how I know it has better time management than I do."
4. "I told my boss I work best under pressure, so now every deadline is yesterday and my chair is a stress ball."
5. "We did a trust fall at the team-building day; HR caught me, then wrote me up for falling."
6. "My calendar is so full of meetings about meetings that I now book a meeting to plan the meeting about the meeting."
7. "The Wi-Fi password is 'productivity2026' — nobody has typed it right since March, which explains a lot."
8. "I brought my own mug so nobody would steal it; someone stole it and left a note saying 'thanks for the mug, great team spirit'."
9. "The meeting room is called the Innovation Lab, which is where good ideas go to be scheduled for next quarter."
10. "I asked HR for a raise and they said the best they could do was a pizza party, so now I am the highest-paid slice in the building."

## Selection

**Winner: #1.**

Why it wins against the brief, criterion by criterion:

1. **Real joke, setup + turn.** Setup: "we're a family here" (the most
   recognisable corporate line there is). Turn: asking for an inheritance —
   taking the family metaphor literally — then the punchline lands twice:
   the old laptop (the inheritance) and the PIP (the consequence).
2. **Universally recognisable.** Every office worker has heard "we're a
   family here" from a boss, and every office worker knows a performance
   improvement plan is the polite way of saying "you're about to be fired".
3. **Surprise.** The reader expects a heartwarming or cynical family line;
   nobody expects an inheritance claim, and nobody expects the PIP as the
   punchline — the joke escalates twice instead of once.
4. **Punchline last, every word earning its place.** 137 characters, one line,
   the last phrase ("performance improvement plan") is the laugh.
5. **Fresh.** The "we're a family" trope is common, but the inheritance +
   PIP combination is not a joke going round the office — it literalises the
   metaphor in a direction nobody has mined.

Runners-up: #5 (trust fall + HR write-up, tight at 89 chars, but the trust-fall
premise is well-worn) and #8 (stolen mug + "great team spirit" note, good
second turn, but the setup is longer than the laugh). #3 and #6 are
complaints stated plainly with a weak turn, which the brief explicitly
penalises. #10's "highest-paid slice" is a decent pun but the pizza-party
premise is one of the most told office jokes there is.
