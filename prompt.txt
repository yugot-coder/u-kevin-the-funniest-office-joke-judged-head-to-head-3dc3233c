Write one original one-line joke about office life for the make.fun
competition "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head".

Requirements:
- One single line, 1–200 characters, UTF-8 text.
- A real joke with a setup and a turn, built on something anyone who has
  worked in an office recognises (e.g. "we're a family here", meetings,
  reply-all, the printer).
- Surprise: a turn the reader does not see coming, not a plain complaint.
- Punchline last, every word earning its place.
- Fresh: not a joke already going round the office.

Write 8–10 original candidates in CANDIDATES.md, pick the strongest
against the judge brief, and make main.py (standard library only,
deterministic) write exactly that joke to /output/entry.txt with a
validate_entry() gate check (1–200 characters, not only white space).
